## 1. Executive Summary

DhakaFin is a **one-stop finance & compliance partner for Bangladeshi SMEs and individuals**.
We combine five service lines — tax, VAT, audit support, cost-efficiency review, and
accounting — into retainer-based bundles, so a client never has to juggle five different
firms for five different problems.

**The core problem we solve:** Most SMEs in Dhaka handle compliance reactively — filing
late, overpaying tax/VAT out of fear or ignorance, getting surprise audit findings, and
losing money through procurement leakage they never see. Existing solutions are
fragmented (one accountant for books, one agent for VAT, a CA firm only at year-end)
and priced opaquely.

**Our wedge (what's different):**
1. **Bundled, transparent pricing** — packages with published starting prices (rare in this market).
2. **Cost-efficiency / leakage audit as a branded product** ("CostGuard") — almost no local
   firm markets forensic procurement review to SMEs, yet every owner worries about "purchase e churi hocche ki na".
3. **Tech-forward client experience** — client portal, deadline reminders, bilingual (Bangla + English) communication.

**Business model:** Tier-1 one-off services generate volume and leads → converted into
Tier-2 monthly retainers (the revenue backbone) → upsold into Tier-3 advisory and
cost-efficiency projects (the margin).

**Year-1 goal (illustrative):** 40 retainer clients, ~৳50–60 lakh revenue, break-even by month 9–12.

## 3. Market Context (Bangladesh)

*(Qualitative overview — verify figures before publication)*

- **Compliance is becoming unavoidable.** NBR's e-return system, expanding mandatory
  e-filing (already required for companies), the Income Tax Act 2023 (replacing the 1984
  Ordinance), and the VAT & SD Act 2012 regime mean SMEs can no longer "wing it".
- **Supply side is fragmented.** Big-4-affiliated firms serve corporates; hundreds of
  small practitioners serve SMEs with inconsistent quality, opaque pricing, and zero
  client-experience thinking. Nobody owns the "SME compliance partner" brand in Dhaka.
- **SMEs under-invest in controls.** Procurement fraud and expense leakage are common
  owner complaints, but forensic reviews are marketed as big-company services. A
  productized, affordable version is an open lane.
- **Seasonality we can exploit:** individual return season peaks around **Tax Day (~30 Nov)**;
  June-financial-year companies run audits roughly **July–October**; VAT is monthly (returns
  due around the 15th). Marketing calendar should mirror this.

### Recommended additions beyond your original 5
1. **Company secretarial / RJSC compliance** — natural adjacency; every audit surfaces filing lapses; cheap to deliver, very sticky.
2. **Business formation & licensing** — every new company we form is a ready-made retainer prospect.
3. **Virtual CFO & payroll** — monetizes the data we already maintain; moves us up the value chain.

### ⚠️ Critical dependency: audit signing
Statutory audits of companies must be **signed by an ICAB member (CA)**. Unless a founder/partner
holds an ICAB practice license, DhakaFin's model is:
- DhakaFin delivers **audit support** (books finalization, schedules, audit-ready files), and
- a **partnered CA firm signs** the audit (we take a referral/servicing margin).
This must be settled *before* the website promises "audit" services — wording matters legally.
**Decision needed: in-house CA, partnership, or pure audit-support model?**

## 8. Operations Plan

**Team (Year 1):**
- Practice lead (founder) — sales, QC, advisory
- Tax manager (1), VAT/compliance officer (1), junior accountants (2–3)
- Company secretary (part-time/retainer)
- Partner CA firm (for audit signing) + tax lawyer (on call)

**Delivery workflow:**
1. Onboard: engagement letter, KYC, document checklist, access to books/NBR credentials.
2. Calendar-driven cycle: every client has a compliance calendar (VAT monthly, RJSC annual, tax season…).
3. Four-eyes QC: senior reviews every filing before submission.
4. Client comms: monthly WhatsApp/email status note; portal (Phase 2).

**Tooling:** Google Workspace, Tally/QuickBooks/Xero, NBR e-return portal, shared deadline
tracker (even a disciplined spreadsheet beats missed deadlines), e-sign + encrypted storage.

**SLAs worth publishing:** filing on time or penalty-advisory free; proposal in 48h; response within 1 business day.

## 10. Competition & SWOT

**Competitive landscape (categories, not names):**
| Category | Their weakness we exploit |
|---|---|
| Big-4 affiliate firms | Too expensive for SMEs; junior-staff delivery |
| Local CA/accounting firms | Opaque pricing, no packages, poor client experience |
| Solo practitioners/agents | Capacity & quality risk, no breadth |
| Online return-filing services | Personal tax only, no business depth |
| In-house accountants | No independence, blind to leakage tricks |

**SWOT**
- **Strengths:** bundled one-stop model; CostGuard differentiator; transparent pricing; tech-forward.
- **Weaknesses:** new brand, no track record; audit-signing dependency on partner CA.
- **Opportunities:** e-filing mandate pushing SMEs to seek help; founders' fraud anxiety; seasonal spikes.
- **Threats:** price undercutting; regulatory change; talent poaching; trust barrier for a new firm (mitigate with founder's-face branding, testimonials, published SLAs).
